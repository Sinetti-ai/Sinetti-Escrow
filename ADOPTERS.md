# Adopters

Organisations and projects that run, integrate, or build on Sinetti Escrow.
Each entry needs a publicly verifiable reference: a URL, an article, or an
on-chain address. Anyone aware of an adoption may add it by pull request.

There are no adopters yet. The contracts are on a public testnet, unaudited, and
not for real funds; the first entries here will be testnet experiments.

| Adopter | Type | How they use it | Reference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

Types follow the
[LF Decentralized Trust definition of adopters](https://lf-decentralized-trust.github.io/governance/governing-documents/defining-adopters/):
ecosystem participant, service provider, upstream or downstream project, end
user, consultancy.
